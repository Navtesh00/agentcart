## Vision
Commerce shifting clicks -> conversations. Transactions still outside experience. Razorpay brings payments into AI-native journeys — transactions at speed of intent.

## Suite — 3 surfaces
1. **In-App Commerce** — embed agentic payment inside app, contextual checkout without leaving experience. Live in Beta.
2. **On LLMs** — beyond visibility on LLM platforms, complete purchase directly within conversation (ChatGPT Apps, Claude). Turn AI discovery into secure in-chat transaction. Built on NPCI + OpenAI.
3. **Voice AI** — discover/decide/pay via voice, retain auth/compliance.

## Rails for Agentic Era
- **Agentic Methods**: UPI Reserve Pay (Live) — consent-based pre-authorized within approved spending limits (SBMD Single Block Multi Debit). UPI Circle (coming soon) — delegated/shared auth.
- **AI-Ready MCP & APIs**: 40+ composable tools & APIs to build/orchestrate intelligence & act — Intelligent payment flows across agentic surfaces.
- **Full-Stack Payments**: UPI/Cards/Wallets/Netbanking
- **Risk & Compliance**: real-time fraud detection, authentication, PCI DSS L1, granular controls pre-set limits + delegated + visibility, scalable low-latency.
